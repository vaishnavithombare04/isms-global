Intakes Available in Australia

In contrast with one intake in Indian universities, Australian universities generally offer multiple major intakes per academic year:

  • Intake 1: February / March (Primary Intake)
  • Intake 2: July / August (Secondary Intake)
  • Intake 3: September / October (Selected Courses)

Average Annual Tuition Fees

  • Undergraduate: AUD 20,000 to AUD 45,000 / year
  • Postgraduate: AUD 22,000 to AUD 50,000 / year
  • MBA Programs: AUD 30,000 to AUD 60,000 / year
  • Ph.D. Programs: AUD 30,000 to AUD 60,000 (Grants up to 100%)

Australia Student Visa Requirements (Subclass 500)

You will have access to stay in Australia for the length of time it takes to finish your study if you hold a Subclass 500 student visa.

Valid Passport
Enrolment Confirmation (CoE)
Academic Credentials
English Scores (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL)
Proof of Financial Funds
OSHC Health Cover
