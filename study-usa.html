Module Shell 01

F-1 Student Visa & Work Rights Framework

F-1 visa rules allow international students to study full-time in the US. Post-study work rights include:

  • SEVIS I-20 Document: Issued by accredited US universities
  • CPT (Curricular Practical Training): Off-campus internships during study
  • OPT (Optional Practical Training): 12-month post-graduation work permit
  • STEM OPT Extension: Additional 24-month work extension for eligible STEM fields
Module Shell 02

Academic System & Intakes

US higher education institutions operate under semester/quarter systems with flexible credit transfers:

  • Fall Intake (Aug/Sep): Primary intake with maximum course & scholarship availability
  • Spring Intake (Jan/Feb): Secondary intake offered across key faculties
  • Degree Types: Bachelor's (4 Years), Master's (1.5 - 2 Years), PhD / Doctorate
  • Assistantships: GRA (Graduate Research) & GTA (Graduate Teaching) stipends
