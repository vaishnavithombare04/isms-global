Quick Facts and Education System

Studying in Dubai is good because you get to go to universities from all around the world and you can also get a job in a city that is growing very fast. The schools in Dubai work with universities from other countries they have many different programs that you can choose from, and they have very good places to learn. Dubai is a rapidly expanding urban centre worldwide and is ideal for entrepreneurial endeavours; thus, there will be new positions consistently emerging.

Education System in Dubai

Dubai has an education system that is in line with international standards:

  • The higher education system in Dubai focuses on learning.
  • The main goal is to help students develop practical skills.
  • Research and innovation are also important.
  • Students get direct industry exposure.
  • This helps significantly with getting a job after graduation.

Types of Institutions in Dubai

International Branch Campuses Well-known universities from around the world have established official campuses in Dubai.
Local Universities Dubai has local universities that offer different undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Intakes Available in Dubai

Universities in Dubai usually have intakes all year round, providing flexibility for applicants:

September Intake: The main time to start because you have the most options for courses.
January Intake: Popular intake and many students like to start their courses at this time.
May Intake: Available for select courses at some universities in Dubai.

Job Prospects & Employment

Careers, Part-Time Work, and Student Life

Dubai has a lot of career opportunities in many different fields. Dubai is a place where people can really build a career. According to reports on the job market, Dubai keeps attracting companies and talented professionals from all over the world.

Finance & Business

Dubai is a world hub for business and an excellent place to study finance and business with multinational corporate headquarters.

Information Technology

Dubai's tech sector is growing fast. This means there are job chances for people who work in technology, software, and AI.

Hospitality & Tourism

The availability of and level of complexity of medical & tourism services in the UAE are quickly improving, increasing job availability.

Logistics & Aviation

The aviation and global logistics industry in Dubai helps people build careers around the world through major airlines & ports.

Healthcare

The healthcare sector in the UAE keeps getting bigger and better. As a result, there are more career opportunities in healthcare.

Marketing & Digital Media

Digital marketing and media sector is developing rapidly. Now is a great period for people interested in this sphere.

Part-Time Job Opportunities & Internships

Many students who study in Dubai have an opportunity to get internship experience. Students may find some part-time jobs in Dubai. The possibilities depend on the conditions of their student visa and university. Students must obey these conditions while looking for part-time jobs.

Life in Dubai & FAQs

Student Lifestyle and Communities

The student life in Dubai is very lively, contemporary, and fun, with a number of different nationalities and ethnicities living throughout the city.

Entertainment and Leisure

There are several entertaining and leisure activities for students available in Dubai, namely:

  • Shopping at world-famous big malls
  • Going to pristine sandy beaches
  • Attending international cultural festivals
  • Watching global sports events
  • Eating at international restaurants and cafes
  • Using the clean public transit (metro, buses & taxis)

Student Communities

There are many activities that universities offer including clubbing, networking, physical activities, and cultural programs.
