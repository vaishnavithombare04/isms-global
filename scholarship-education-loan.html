Financial documentation is crucial for visa applications. Students may need:

  • Bank statements
  • Education loan documents
  • Sponsor letters
  • Scholarship letters
  • Proof of income

There are certain countries such as the UK where it is necessary to show that you can afford the tuition costs and the cost of living.
