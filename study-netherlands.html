Study in the Netherlands

With Netherlands welcoming students from 160 countries, you get to experience an international culture in Dutch universities. This culture is supported by an education system that focuses on students and is also connected to industries.

Keyword: Study in Netherlands, Study in Netherlands for international students, study abroad, Netherlands universities, masters in Netherlands for international students.

Why study in the Netherlands?

Pursue your studies in top-notch universities in the Netherlands which offer highly structured courses along with infrastructure at par with international standards. Succeed academically with a pedagogic approach that lays stress on teamwork so that you get an opportunity to associate with your fellow countrymen and international students in a teaching methodology that encourages critical thinking skills applicable to the real-world scenario. Learn about practical aspects embedded in the curriculum and benefit from strong industry relations of universities of the Netherlands with both national and international firms.

Quick Facts and Education System

The Netherlands has an education system that is well-regarded within Europe, featuring clear university tracks and post-study opportunities.

Quick Facts

🌍

Multicultural Country

One in every 10 university students is an international student, welcoming learners from 160 countries.

🛂

Post Study Work Visa

Post Study Work Visa (Orientation Year / Zoekjaar) up to 1 Year for international graduates.

🏙️

Amsterdam Student City

Amsterdam, the capital city, is among the best student cities worldwide for quality of life.

🇬🇧

2000+ English Programs

Over 2,000 English-taught programs to choose from across bachelor's and master's degrees.

💶

Economical Expenses

Economical cost of education and living expenses compared to other major study abroad countries.

🛡️

One of the Safest Countries

Recognized as one of the safest countries in the world (2018 Global Peace Index).

💼

Excellent Career Opportunities

The Netherlands offers excellent career opportunities and corporate connections to international students.

Types of Higher Education Institutions

Research Universities Such universities emphasize theoretical and research-oriented education.
Universities of Applied Sciences Universities of applied science emphasize practical learning, internships, and job readiness.

Degree Structure in the Netherlands

  • 🎓Bachelor’s Degrees: A bachelor’s degree typically takes 3 to 4 years.
  • 📜Master’s Degrees: A master’s degree takes approximately 1 to 2 years.
  • 🔬PhD Degrees: A PhD program mainly emphasizes advanced research.

Destinations & Living

Student Cities, Tuition Costs, and Scholarships

The Netherlands has great student cities that balance excellent academics with a high-quality international lifestyle.

Student Cities in the Netherlands

🇳🇱 Amsterdam

Famous for excellent universities, international atmosphere, job opportunities, and vibrant student lifestyle.

🇳🇱 Rotterdam

Famous for its premier programs related to business, global logistics, maritime trade, and architecture.

🇳🇱 Utrecht

A perfect balance between a student's social lifestyle and rigorous academic research.

🇳🇱 Eindhoven

Famous worldwide for high technology, engineering, hardware innovation, and design.

🇳🇱 Groningen

A classic university city known for a huge student population and vibrant cultural events.

🇳🇱 Leiden

Famous for rich academic history, law programs, and world-leading scientific research studies.

What is the Cost of Studying in the Netherlands?

The Netherlands is an affordable place to get an education compared to some other popular study destinations. Average tuition fees:

Undergraduate Programs Bachelor's degrees (Per Year)
€6,000 to €15,000
Postgraduate Programs Master's degrees (Per Year)
€8,000 to €20,000
MBA Programs Master of Business Administration
€20,000 to €40,000

* Students should also consider living expenses: accommodation, public transit, food, healthcare insurance, and personal spending.

Scholarships & Part-Time Work

Scholarships help lessen financial burden and make studying abroad accessible:

  • 🇳🇱 Government Scholarships
  • 🏛️ University Scholarships
  • 🔬 Research Scholarships
  • 📚 Program Scholarships
Part-Time Job Advantages: International students have the option to work part-time during studies per visa laws. Benefits include additional income, work experience, building professional relationships through networking, and skill development.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why do studies in Netherlands? +

Globally recognized education, innovation in teaching methodologies, and great career opportunities make the Netherlands the right choice.

2. Are there scholarships available in the Netherlands? +

Scholarships are offered by both the government and universities to international students.

3. Can foreign students work in the Netherlands? +

International students are permitted to work part-time in accordance with visa requirements.

4. Is Netherlands safe for international students? +

Yes, the Netherlands is known as one of the safest nations for foreign students.

Closing Thoughts on Studying in the Netherlands

The Netherlands is a place for students who want to study business, engineering, technology, healthcare, logistics or environmental science. The Netherlands gives students a lot of chances to learn and get better at their jobs. Before they apply, students should look carefully at the universities, the courses they can take, the scholarships they can get, how much it costs to live in the Netherlands, and what kind of jobs they can get in the future. This will help them make informed choices about what they want to study.
