Study in France

The country of France enjoys one of the best educational systems across the globe. No matter what fields or degrees you aim at studying, there is no shortage of universities and institutions in France that will provide the best environment for your studies.

There are countless benefits for students who choose to study in France. These include top-notch research laboratories, experienced faculty members working for your success and world-renowned institutions that have exceptional abilities when it comes to teaching and learning process. Paris, being the best city for pursuing higher studies in France, is also a city where innovations happen and many startups are run by smart and enterprising people of the region.

Key Benefits

Education Advantages and Career Environment

France has many advantages for international students looking for quality education and global career opportunities.

1. Globally Recognized Education

French universities and institutions are respected everywhere for quality education and research. Degrees are recognized around the world and valued by employers.

2. Low Tuition Fees

Education in France is not very expensive. Tuition fees at public universities in France are low compared to many other study abroad destinations.

3. Interesting Life & Culture

France is famous for its history, beautiful art, great fashion, amazing architecture and delicious food. International students really enjoy living in France.

4. Job Opportunities

France has many jobs available across Business, Finance, Luxury Brand Management, Hospitality, Engineering, IT, Fashion & Healthcare.

5. Research & Innovation

France is recognized for its excellence in research and innovation across various scientific, tech, and academic fields.

6. Strategic European Location

Students can easily travel across Schengen European countries while studying in France.

7. Multicultural Environment

France welcomes students from all over the world and supports cultural diversity in academic and social life.

8. High Quality of Life

France has modern transport, great healthcare facilities, generous discounts for students, and top public amenities.

Academic System

French Education System and Degree Structure

France is one of those countries with the best educational system in all of Europe, offering specialized institution tracks for all career goals.

Higher Education Institution Types

Public Universities

Provide low-cost, state-subsidized education in various academic fields.

Grandes Écoles

Prestigious elite schools offering courses in business, engineering, administration, and politics.

Specialized Schools

Dedicated institutions for fashion, art, hotel design, and culinary arts educational systems.

Degree Structure in France

  • Bachelor’s Programs: Undergraduate courses that typically take three years to complete.
  • Master’s Programs: Postgraduate qualifications that usually last two years.
  • Doctoral Programs: PhD courses that prioritize advanced research and innovation.

Intakes in France

France provides students with multiple intakes during the academic year:

September Intake: It is the major intake and the time when the largest number of courses are open.
January Intake: Open to certain courses and institutions. You must start preparing for admissions 8 to 12 months before your desired intake.

Scholarships to Study in France

Scholarships lower a student's financial burden and increase access to studying abroad:

  • Government Scholarships: Provided by the French government for meritorious foreign students.
  • University Scholarships: Offered directly by French universities and Grandes Écoles.
  • Eiffel Excellence Scholarship: Prestigious scholarship for international master's and doctorate students.
  • Research Scholarships: Financial support for doctoral and research scholars with specific deadlines.

Life as a Student in France

Life as a student in France is filled with excitement, rich culture and friendliness. Students have access to many cultural experiences throughout their time in France:

Museums and art galleries
Historical landmarks
Fashion & cultural festivals
Cafes and gourmet restaurants
Public transit (trains, buses, metro)
University clubs, sports & events

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why study in France? +

World class education, low tuition fees, great career prospects and cultural experiences – that’s what France offers.

2. What are the popular courses in France? +

Among the most popular programs are Business Management, Fashion Design, Hospitality, Engineering, Information Technology, and Culinary Arts.

3. What are the main intakes in France? +

Main intakes in France are in September and January.

4. Do you offer scholarships in France? +

Yes, government and university scholarships (including the Eiffel Excellence Scholarship) are available to international students.

5. Are international students allowed to work in France? +

Yes, as per the visa regulations, students are allowed to work part-time while studying.
