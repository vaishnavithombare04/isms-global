Popular Study Destinations

Unlock educational opportunities in countries recognized worldwide for academic rigor and career outcomes.

UK

United Kingdom

Home to legendary universities and a 2-year post-study work visa (Graduate Route).

  • Intakes: Sept, Jan, May
  • Average Tuition: £12,000 - £25,000/yr
  • Work Permit: Up to 2 Years
Explore UK
USA

United States

Global epicenter for tech, scientific research, and 3-year STEM OPT extensions.

  • Intakes: August, January
  • Average Tuition: $20,000 - $45,000/yr
  • Work Permit: 1 to 3 Years (OPT)
Explore USA
IE

Ireland

Europe's silicon hub with immense opportunities in IT, Pharma, and Finance.

  • Intakes: September, January
  • Average Tuition: €10,000 - €22,000/yr
  • Work Permit: 2 Years (Stay Back)
Explore Ireland
AU

Australia

High standard of living, pristine environment, and generous post-study stay-back pathways.

  • Intakes: Feb, July, November
  • Average Tuition: A$22,000 - A$40,000/yr
  • Work Permit: 2 to 4 Years
Explore Australia
