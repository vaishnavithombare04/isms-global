Personalized Counselling

Our experienced counsellors help students find the career path. They look at a student’s

background, interests, strengths, and future goals. We help students choose the course and

study destination for a successful career.

What Does a One-on-One Session with ISMS Global Education Experts Include

Our personalized counselling sessions help students understand their studies. During the

session students get:

  • Career and profile evaluation
  • Advice on the countries and universities
  • Course recommendations based on career goals
  • Information on admission requirements and eligibility
  • Scholarship and financial planning advice
  • Visa guidance and application process support
  • Details about -study work opportunities
  • A personalized study abroad plan

Our counsellors make sure each student gets guidance that fits their goals and preferences.

They help students make decisions about their career and education.

Additional Services by ISMS Global Education Include:

  • Pre-departure orientation program
  • Advice on foreign exchange and travel
  • Advice on educational loans
  • Help with accommodation arrangements
  • Resume writing and interview tips
  • Internship and career advice
  • Advice on alumni support and networking
  • Information on travel and health insurance

Tips for Optimum Benefit of Individual Counselling Session

To maximize your benefit from personal counselling by our team of study abroad experts,

you must do the following:

  • Do thorough research before visiting the counsellor
  • Prepare a list of questions and concerns
  • Know your objectives in life and your budget
  • Have an honest discussion about your education background
  • Discuss scholarship issues freely
  • Be open-minded regarding opportunities abroad
  • Note down important information for future use

This will allow you to get maximum benefit out of the session

What Documents Should I Bring

Bring the following documents/information during your session:

  • Academic transcripts & mark sheets
  • Photocopy of passport (if available)
  • Updated resume/CV
  • English test score (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL)
  • If available work experience document (if applicable)

List of preferred countries and courses Inquiries about admissions, scholarships, visas and

careers Your counsellor will be better prepared to offer advice if you have these ready.
