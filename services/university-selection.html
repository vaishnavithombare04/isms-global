Decision Flow & Evaluation Criteria

How Shortlisting Works

We translate your academic background into a tailored selection matrix matching top global institutions.

1

Profile Fit Evaluation

Assess the student's background, core interests, individual strengths, and extracurricular achievements to define suitable target study destinations.

2

Academic Fit Matching

Match the student's GPA, academic transcripts, prerequisite subject background, and English language test scores against official university admission requirements.

3

Financial Fit & Budgeting

Evaluate total cost of education including tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses, and eligibility for merit-based scholarships or education loans.

4

Career Fit & Outcome Alignment

Align selected programs with industry demand, post-study work visa policies, internship opportunities, and long-term career aspirations.

CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED

Approved university catalogue, current courses, specific admission criteria, intake dates, tuition fees, scholarship data, partnership evidence, and authorised university logos must be confirmed prior to production deployment.
