Visa Preparation

Application for a student visa is one of the most crucial parts of the entire study abroad

process. There are unique requirements when it comes to each country, and at times it

becomes quite challenging for students to cope with the visa application process. In such

situations, our company, ISMS Global Education, assists students in preparing their visa

applications for international travel.

Our team of expert visa consultants assist students in preparing the entire visa process from

the beginning to the end.

When Should I Apply for My Student Visa

If your course offers you admission, make sure you receive the relevant university

confirmation documents such as your CAS (UK), COE (Australia), I-20 (USA), or other official

letters. Then, immediately after, make sure you apply for your student visa. It is good to

remember that depending on the destination country and admission semester, your student

visa processing time may vary. Applying early is a safe way to give yourself some time to

settle accommodation, travel, or other visa and entry procedures hassle-free.

How Do I Apply for a Student Visa

The student visa process involves the following:

  • Obtain your letter of offer and acceptance!
  • Get the necessary papers together!
  • Prepare financial documents and proof of funds.
  • Fill in your visa application online.
  • Pay your visa fees.
  • Take your biometric data or attend a visa interview at the embassy or visa application

center.

  • Submit your visa application form.
  • Wait for the visa to be processed.

Since every country has their own student visa rules and documents, having proper support

in the process is really important.

Step 15: Application for Student Visa

Once admission is confirmed, a student visa can be applied for.

  • Student Visa Documentation
  • Passport
  • Offer letter
  • Visa application form
  • Financial documents
  • English language scores
  • Tuberculosis test reports (for some countries)
  • Passport-size photographs

Step 16: Prepare for Visa Interview

Visa interviews may be held in some countries.

Students need to:

  • Have knowledge about course structure
  • Have career plans clear in mind
  • Prove that they have financial capacity
  • Prove that they have study purposes

Self-confidence and truthfulness are vital.
