Pre-Departure and Post-Arrival Briefing

Our main objective is to provide you with all the necessary support at every step during your

study abroad experience. Preparing yourself well for overseas education is important to

ensure that you excel. Our counsellors will help you get ready by providing you with all the

information you require.

What Does the Pre-Departure Briefing Include

Travel and Flight Guidance

We guide students on things to do before they travel. This includes:

  • Flight booking tips
  • Baggage
  • Important papers you need for travel
  • Airport procedures
  • Immigration Guidance
  • Accommodation Guidance

We also help students with:

  • Student housing options
  • Temporary accommodation
  • Shared apartments and private housing
  • Safety and location considerations
  • Financial & Forex Guidance

We help students understand:

  • How to exchange currency and use forex services
  • How international banking works
  • Using debit and credit cards, in countries
  • Managing your expenses. Making a budget
  • We make sure students know about currency exchange and forex services
  • Students learn about banking and debit/credit card usage abroad

Health and Insurance Information

Students will be provided with advice about:

  • Requirements for health insurance
  • Medical support services
  • Emergency contacts
  • Healthcare system in the host country

Culture and Lifestyle Preparation

Living abroad entails adaptation to a completely new cultural environment. Therefore, we

assist students with:

  • The local culture and traditions
  • Climate and necessary clothing items
  • The local transport system
  • Communication and manners
  • Student life in a foreign country

Academic Preparation

We provide students with information about:

  • Orientation programs at the university
  • Classroom culture and pedagogical practices
  • Coursework and attendance requirements
  • Academic management and planning

Safety and Emergency Preparation

Students will be advised about:

  • Personal safety practices
  • Phone numbers of emergency hotlines
  • Laws and regulations
  • Safety during travels and abroad

Things to Pack: The "Must-Have" Packing Guide

ISMS Global Education offers a complete student pack including guidance on.

  • Documents that need to be carried by the student
  • Clothes required by the student
  • Electricals and accessories that the student will need
  • Medicines and personal belongings that the student might require
  • Items that students ought not to pack

What is in The Post-Arrival Briefing

  • Arrival at Airport & Initial Assistance
  • With available provisions, the student will be informed about:
  • Airport pick-up facility
  • Availability of transportation
  • Accessing accommodation safely
  • Orientation at Accommodation

Students will be provided with information on:

  • Activating local SIM cards
  • Setting up of student accounts with local banks
  • Online banking facilities and support for transfers, etc
  • Services available for international money transfers
  • University Enrolment
  • The students will be informed on:
  • University admissions and registration procedures
  • Collecting students’ ID cards
  • Locating their respective timetables
  • Getting oriented at university during orientation week

Page No: 8 Pre-Departure Support

We're here to assist you in preparing for your study-abroad journey.

Countdown Begins! The idea of moving to a different nation to pursue education is very

fascinating to each student. Nevertheless, there are several stages that need to be

completed even when one receives admission and gets a student visa.

Pre-departure is a very important phase, as a student is required to prepare for the process

of studying abroad. All the preparations like booking accommodation, transport, adapting

to cultural changes, and money management are to be done.

Topics Covered in Pre-Departure Assistance

  • Visa and Immigration guidance

This is the most important part of pre-departure assistance, and it entails that the student is

fully aware of the visa and immigration regulations.

Advice includes:

  • Student visa guidelines
  • Airport immigration procedures
  • Travel documentation required
  • Customs rules
  • Entrance guidelines
  • Accommodation Services

It is crucial that accommodation is found to provide safety and comfort to international

students.

Students will be assisted before departure to help understand the following types of

accommodation:

  • University accommodation
  • Dormitory
  • Shared flat
  • Private accommodation
  • Homestay

In addition, students will get advice on how to:

  • Make rent agreements
  • Ensure security
  • Find transportation
  • Settle utility bills
  • Access Internet

Early reservations make it easier for students to find better and cheaper accommodation.

  • Travel Arrangement Services

Making travel arrangements will also be a significant aspect of a planned education abroad.

Students will be provided with guidance in the future.

  • Flight reservation
  • Baggage arrangements
  • Airports processes
  • Transit details
  • Travel insurance

All international students will be suggested to arrive a couple of days prior to orientation at

university. It will be also significant for students to hold travel insurance due to possible

unforeseen circumstances.

  • Financial Planning Assistance

Proper financial management is highly critical for successful international education.

Preparation services will assist students in understanding:

  • Tuition payments
  • Expenses
  • Currency conversion
  • International banking
  • Budgeting
  • Cultural Knowledge and Lifestyle Training

Moving to another nation implies embracing a new culture and surroundings.

Preparation services can aid international students in comprehending:

  • Culture
  • Etiquette
  • Public behavior
  • Differences between cultures
  • Climate

Those preparing to study at universities in the UK for international students typically gain

insights into British culture and academics prior to their departure.

  • Packing Tips

Many foreign students get puzzled while deciding what they should pack prior to going to

another country.

Advice before departure will assist the students to pack important things such as:

  • Educational documents
  • Clothing suitable for weather conditions
  • Medical supplies and medications
  • Electronics
  • Personal items

It is always recommended not to take extra items on board.

  • Emotional and Psychological Preparation

It can be emotionally difficult to leave the family behind and move abroad.

Pre-departure preparation will help the student psychologically get ready for:

  • Homesickness
  • Being independent
  • Managing time
  • Socializing
  • Self-confidence

Contacting existing students before leaving is one way that students can get prepared.

Final Thoughts:

International educational preparation requires pre-departure support as its most essential

component. International student experience requires proper planning because it helps

students transition into their new academic and cultural surroundings. The complete

process from travel arrangements to accommodation and financial planning and emotional

preparation enables students to have successful experience during their study abroad

program.

Students should start their study abroad preparation process by creating an organized

system which they will follow throughout their entire study abroad period while reaching out

to experts when they require assistance.

Through ISMS Global Education expert support, students can start their international

education path which will lead them to achieve success in their global professional future.
