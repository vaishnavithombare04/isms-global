Standing by your side until the end

Welcome to the incredible journey of pursuing your studies abroad. Prepare to embark on

an adventurous journey where you will make new friends, acquire new knowledge, develop

new skills, and have amazing experiences. Though the initial adjustment period in a new

environment may require some time, once you adapt, you will find that it is a very enriching

experience.

Our counseling services include everything you need, ranging from advice on how to arrange

your accommodation, financial advice, and assistance with the migration process. Your

wellness and happiness are always our main priorities.

We also collaborate with professional psychologists, in case you require any psychological

assistance during the whole journey. It is your opportunity to build your own amazing

success story.

Need for Proper Arrival Assistance

Adjusting to life in a foreign country may be challenging for international students. The

unfamiliar environment, culture, transport system, and personal adjustments may cause

confusion in the early stages.

Arrival assistance can help students:

  • Adjusting to a new country
  • Learn about its systems and transport
  • Get to their accommodation
  • Handle university processes
  • Get accustomed to different cultures
  • Gain confidence and security

Proper arrival assistance is essential for those enrolled in educational programs abroad.

University Orientation Programs

International students who arrive at study abroad university institutions receive orientation

programs which most universities provide.

Orientation sessions help students understand:

  • Campus facilities
  • Academic systems
  • Student support services
  • Library access
  • Health and safety guidelines
  • Classroom expectations & many more

Students receive chances to connect with their peers while meeting professors and

university personnel. Orientation programs provide students with two benefits because they

help students develop their self-assurance and establish their university routine.

Establishing Necessary Services

Once students have arrived, there are a few necessary things they should set up.

Necessary Actions After Arriving

Create a Bank Account

Foreign students are likely to require bank accounts to cover their basic costs and

paychecks from any part-time jobs.

Buy a Local SIM Card

This is helpful so that students can communicate more easily with their university friends

and friends.

Registration with Healthcare Providers

Students should familiarize themselves with the healthcare and insurance procedures of

the new country.

Arrange Transportation Passes

Final Thoughts

The arrival process serves as a crucial milestone that international students must complete

during their educational journey. Students need proper planning together with guidance to

adapt successfully to their new country and university environment and their new way of life.

The complete educational experience abroad begins with airport procedures and

accommodation arrangements and continues through academic preparation and cultural

adaptation. Students exploring study abroad opportunities should stay organized, remain

open to new experiences, and seek support whenever needed.

International students can start their global education journey with confidence when they

receive professional guidance from the ISMS Global Education department, which helps

them maximize their university experience.
