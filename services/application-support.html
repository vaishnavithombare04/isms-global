Source excerpt 1

Applying for an overseas university will become simpler when you understand the admission

criteria and break down the application process into steps.

At ISMS Global Education, we take care of our students at every step of the way they apply

to universities abroad.

Following are the essential steps that you should consider when applying to a university

abroad:

  • Research And Selection of Your Course & Country

The very first step is to choose the type of education you would like to pursue and in what

country. Determine your area of interest. Narrow down on countries according to your

budget Research universities which offer the specific course.

  • Shortlisting of Universities

The universities that match your education level and your affordability have to be selected

when the subject and the place of your studies are known. University ranking and reputation

Course syllabus Entry requirements Cost of education It is wise to select between four to six

universities for acceptance into the college.

  • Eligibility & Requirement

Each college has its own set of rules that should be strictly adhered to by all those who wish

to apply for admission to the college. Each rule is extremely essential and therefore should

be strictly observed. Educational qualification (Transcript of class 10th, 12th, and

graduation). English language proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, and PTE).

  • Submission of Key Documents

Documentation is an essential part of your application process. The documents that you will

require to include are:

  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • Letter of Recommendation (LOR)
  • Transcripts of Your Academic Results
  • CV/Resume
  • Copy of Your Passport

Our expert help at ISMS Global Education will help you write a strong SOP and LOR, which

will improve your chances of getting in.

  • Taking English Language Proficiency Tests

All universities will demand proof regarding your proficiency in oral and written language of

that country. Proofs that can fulfill this requirement are IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo, etc.

  • Submission of Applications

Now is the time to apply for the universities on your list. In the application process, you must

provide all the documents that the university demands. You must pay the required

application fees. The candidate can check the status of the application using the application

tracking system. The probability of acceptance and scholarships will be increased if you

apply earlier.

  • Receiving an Offer Letter

After the application review process, universities will send you the following documents. The

Conditional Offer Letter states that you must fulfill specific requirements to receive the offer.

The Unconditional Offer Letter serves as your final acceptance document.

  • Ensure You Have Adequate Finance & Search for Scholarships

Having sufficient finance should not be just about paying the tuition fee; accommodation,

food, transport, insurance and other expenses must also be considered. International

students must demonstrate their ability to finance their studies before universities grant

them admission. There exist various sources of financial support in the form of scholarships

from universities, governments, and other bodies which might help lower your expenses

considerably. There is often information regarding scholarship opportunities, including

requirements and processes, on the university website.

How to Apply to Universities Abroad

Well done for making the choice of studying abroad! You made a great choice for yourself,

and it will add something new to your life and open up many more doors in the future. First,

the task appears to be extremely difficult.

The thing is that starting from writing a successful application to choosing suitable

universities and sending your application papers on time, you are sure to miss something

important. We understand this very well. This is why our expert advisers have already helped

many other students complete their applications successfully! Do not think that you must

go through all this by yourself!

Now let's look at the steps you need to take to successfully apply to college!

The process of college application requires students to select their desired courses and

their future career paths.

Step 1: Pick your course and what you want to do with your life.

Things to Think About:

  • What kind of job do I want to have in the future
  • Which subjects do I like
  • Which country provides the best opportunities for me
  • What is my budget
  • Do I require internships
  • Do I want to live permanently after that

The selection of the course is very significant for future career options

Top Courses for Studying Abroad

Some of the Trending courses that students pursue while studying abroad include (As

per reports):

  • Business Management
  • MBA
  • Data Analytics
  • Computer Science
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Cyber Security
  • Healthcare Management
  • Nursing
  • Physiotherapy
  • Engineering
  • Digital Marketing
  • Hospitality Management
  • Sports Business Management
  • Finance and Accounting
  • International Business

Students planning a study journey abroad should select courses based on future career

demand and personal interests.

Step 2: Select the Right Overseas University

Once you have selected your course, you can now proceed to select the best overseas

university.

Considerations Before Choosing an Overseas University

  • University Rankings

Research global rankings of the university to get an idea of its prestige.

  • Curriculum of the Course

Learn about the curriculum taught in the course and the practical sessions, internships, and

other opportunities provided.

  • Tuition Cost

Find out the cost of education in various countries.

  • Scholarships

Universities provide scholarships for international students.

  • Location

The location should be conducive to your studies, safe, and easily accessible.

  • Industry Exposure and Placement

Industrial visits and placements are crucial for your future.

  • Work Visa Options

Countries offering post-study work permits are preferred by many students.

Step 3: Eligibility Requirements

Every university abroad has different eligibility requirements

Academic Requirements

Students must meet minimum academic requirements such as:

  • High school completion for bachelor’s programs
  • Bachelor’s degree for master’s programs
  • Minimum percentage or GPA requirements

English Language Requirements

Most universities require proof of English language proficiency.

Popular English tests include:

  • IELTS
  • TOEFL
  • PTE
  • Duolingo English Test

For Study abroad UK admissions, IELTS is commonly accepted.

Standardized Tests

Some universities may require:

  • GRE
  • GMAT
  • SAT
  • ACT

This depends on the course and country

Step 4: Prepare Required Documents

Document preparation is one of the most important parts of the overseas university

application process.

Documents Required for Studying in University Overseas

  • Academic Transcript

Academic transcripts must be provided by applicants.

  • Passport

Passports must be valid while applying internationally.

  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)

SOP contains the following details:

  • Details of your educational background
  • Details about your career goals
  • Details about the choice of the course
  • Details about the selection of the institution

SOP helps increase the chance of admission.

  • Letters of Recommendation (LOR)

LOR should be from:

Teachers/ Professors/ Employers

  • Curriculum Vitae/ Resume

Professional CV must be included in the application.

  • English Score Card

English test scores such as IELTS, TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo are required.

  • Work Experience

For MBA and professional courses, experience certificates are needed.

Step 5: Shortlist Universities

Students should shortlist universities based on

  • Budget
  • Eligibility
  • Rankings
  • Scholarships
  • Career opportunities
  • Location
  • Course structure

It is always recommended to apply to:

  • Dream universities
  • Moderate universities
  • Safe universities

This increases admission chances.

Step 6: Begin Application for Overseas University Studies

With all necessary documents on hand, one may start applying to overseas universities.

Direct University Application

One may apply directly to the university via its website.

UCAS for UK Universities Application

This option applies to the ones applying to undergraduate Study Abroad UK courses.

Using UCAS, one may apply to several UK universities with the help of this site.

Common App

Options for many universities in the USA are important.

Through Education Consultants

Most of the students go for professional assistance while choosing their universities,

preparing documents, and applying.

Step 7: Complete Your Application Form

After deciding to apply to a UK University you will have to:

Many students prefer professional guidance for selecting universities, preparing documents,

and managing applications.

  • Put in the correct information
  • Double-check the spelling
  • Upload the right picture
  • Use a professional email address

Check that everything is correct before submission to ensure the application will not be

delayed due to errors made on your application form.

Step 8: Pay Application Fees

Many universities charge application fees.

The application fees are different between countries and institutions.

Some universities may waive fees during promotional periods.

Step 9: Monitor Your Application Status

Once submitted, universities generally follow up with:

  • Application confirmation
  • Access to online portals
  • Interview invitation letters
  • Request for further documentation
  • Admissions offer

It is advisable for students to monitor their emails and portals.

Step 10: Attend Interviews (if required)

Some overseas study programs may have interviews.

The interview can be conducted in various ways such as:

Through the internet

Telephone

Personal

Questions during Interview

  • Why do you want to take this course
  • Why have you chosen this university
  • Tell me about your future goals and plans
  • Why should you study abroad
  • How will you arrange expenses
  • Answer all these questions clearly.

Step 11: Get the Offer Letter

On selection, you get an offer

If selected, the university abroad sends an offer letter.

Types of Offer Letters

Conditional Offer Letter

Students must fulfil additional requirements such as:

  • Final academic results
  • English test scores
  • Pending documents

Unconditional Offer Letter

Requirements met and admission secured.

Step 12: Accept the Offer Letter

Acceptance of admission must be done via:

  • Accepting the offer
  • Paying the tuition deposit
  • Submitting required documents

Following this stage, universities release visa documents.

Step 13: Application for Scholarships

Scholarship applications lessen costs and help in affordable studying abroad.

Step 14: Prepare Financial Documentation

Financial documentation is crucial for visa applications.

Students may need:

  • Bank statements
  • Education loan documents
  • Sponsor letters
  • Scholarship letters
  • Proof of income

There are certain countries such as the UK where it is necessary to show that you can afford

the tuition costs and the cost of living.

Step 15: Application for Student Visa

Once admission is confirmed, a student visa can be applied for.

  • Student Visa Documentation
  • Passport
  • Offer letter
  • Visa application form
  • Financial documents
  • English language scores
  • Tuberculosis test reports (for some countries)
  • Passport-size photographs

Step 16: Prepare for Visa Interview

Visa interviews may be held in some countries.

Students need to:

  • Have knowledge about course structure
  • Have career plans clear in mind
  • Prove that they have financial capacity
  • Prove that they have study purposes

Self-confidence and truthfulness are vital.

Step 17: Accommodation Arrangement

Accommodation should be arranged prior to arrival.

Many universities have:

Student accommodation

University accommodations

Flats to share

Private Accommodation

Students can also have:

Sharing flats

Host families

Studio Flats

Location, budget, and safety also should be considered

Step 18: Book Flights & Travel Insurance

Before leaving for your study abroad journey:

  • Attending university orientation sessions
  • Understand local laws and culture
  • Learn about public transport
  • Arrange foreign currency
  • Pack essential items

Students should carry:

  • Passport
  • Visa documents
  • Offer letter
  • Accommodation details
  • Academic documents
  • Emergency contacts
