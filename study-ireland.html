Academic Growth

Why Study in Ireland?

International students who are planning to study in foreign countries and pursue their careers abroad tend to choose Ireland as one of the best places for higher education. As per the latest international education report, Ireland is experiencing significant growth among international student populations due to various factors such as a favorable atmosphere for students, degrees recognized internationally, and increasing employment opportunities. In this comprehensive guide, all that you need to know about studying in Ireland has been provided.

There are many factors that make Ireland an ideal choice for higher education:

🎓

1. Internationally Acclaimed Education System

The quality education provided by Irish universities has earned international recognition and accreditation for Ireland. For students preparing to Study in Ireland, it will provide them with top-notch standards of education, practical learning, and research-oriented studies.

🏢

2. The Base of Leading Multinational Companies

It is the European base of multinational companies such as Google, Microsoft, Meta, Apple, Pfizer, Intel, and Accenture. Therefore, there will be plenty of job opportunities available for international students through internships.

💼

3. Excellent Career Prospects

Students who study in Ireland can find lucrative opportunities in industries such as:

  • • Technology
  • • Health Care
  • • Finance
  • • Pharmaceuticals
  • • Engineering
  • • Data Analytics
  • • Business Management
  • • and many more

4. Friendly and Warm Environment

Irish culture is globally famous for its warm hospitality, vibrant safety standards, and student-friendly communities welcoming international students.

There will be no language issue for the students, as Ireland is an English-speaking nation.

7. Focus on Innovation

Most of the universities in Ireland give special emphasis on innovation and research.

8. Great Cultural Experience

Apart from studies, students will get exposure to Irish culture, which involves music and celebrations.

Monthly Expenses

Cost of Living in Ireland

Maynooth: Very popular for welcoming environment among international students.

The cost of living in Ireland is relatively affordable, though expenses may differ depending on the city. Dublin, the capital, tends to have a higher cost of living compared to other parts of Ireland. However, cities such as Galway, Cork, and Limerick are generally more budget-friendly for international students. The table below provides an overview of the estimated monthly expenses students may incur for essentials such as accommodation, utilities, food, transportation, and entertainment.

Category Expense Description Estimated Cost (€)
1. Housing Dublin / Other Cities / Shared Dublin: €1,200 - €2,200 | Other Cities: €900 - €1,500 | Shared: €500 - €900/mo
2. Utilities Electricity, Heating, Water & Waste €150 - €200 per month
3. Internet & Mobile Broadband & Phone plan Internet: €40 - €60 | Mobile: €15 - €30 per month
4. Food & Groceries Monthly Groceries €250 - €400 per month
5. Transportation Public Transit (Biking/walking common) Dublin: €120 - €150 | Other Cities: €100 - €130 per month
6. Healthcare & Insurance Private Insurance / Dr. Appointment Insurance: €45 - €70/mo | Doctor visit: €60 - €75
7. Recreation & Misc Eating Out / Gym / Movie Eating Out: €12-€20 | Gym: €30-€60/mo | Movie: €10-€15

📜

Third Level Graduate Scheme (24-Month Post-Study Work Permit)

Ireland is advantageous to international students in terms of post-study work due to the Third Level Graduate Scheme, which permits international graduates to work for a maximum period of 24 months. With employment rates being high within these sectors, graduates have a bright future in terms of securing good employment.

✓ Part-time jobs can be done by international students when enrolled (Max 20 hours/week). Benefits: Financial assistance, Work experience, Professional networks, Improved communication skills.
