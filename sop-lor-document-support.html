Statement of Purpose

3. Statement of Purpose (SOP)

SOP contains the following details:

Educational Background

Details of your educational background, academic achievements, projects, and foundational preparation.

Career Goals

Details about your short-term and long-term career goals and how the qualification empowers your aspirations.

Choice of the Course

Details about the choice of the course, curriculum alignment, modules, and specific subject interest.

Selection of Institution

Details about the selection of the institution, university faculty, campus resources, and academic environment.

SOP helps increase the chance of admission.

Recommender Sources  ·  Source: Client PDF p.37

Letters of Recommendation

4. Letters of Recommendation (LOR)

LOR should be from:

Teachers

School teachers or subject tutors familiar with your academic discipline and work ethic.

Professors

University faculty, department heads, or research guides who evaluated your higher education coursework.

Employers

Workplace managers, supervisors, or team leads who can attest to your professional performance and skills.

Privacy & Authentic Endorsement Note: Recommender contact details and official letterheads must be authentic and verified according to institutional submission policies.
