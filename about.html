About Hero and Organisation Introduction

About ISMS Global Edu

ISMS Global Education is a leader in global education services, helping people achieve their international education goals over 17 years.

For over 17 years, ISMS Global Education has provided overseas student support and guidance to young people across the globe through our certified, accredited British council consultants. At ISMS Global Education, we provide complete support throughout the study abroad process — from selecting the right course and university to applications, visa guidance, and pre-departure assistance.

We help students make informed and confident decisions for their academic and career goals. We offer free counselling services to ensure students receive reliable guidance and access to quality international education opportunities. ISMS collaborates with many international colleges and universities.

What We Do, Student Placement, and IELTS Support

What We Do, Student Placement, and IELTS Support

What We Do

We help throughout the entire study abroad process, including career counselling, choosing a university, applying to universities, visa assistance, and pre-departure support.

Student Placement

ISMS Global Education combines industry insights with tailored counselling to make students' study abroad dreams a reality. We work with top universities, international education opportunities, and expert support to make the admission process easy and accessible for students.

We work with reputed universities and institutions across UK, Ireland, Australia, USA, New Zealand, Dubai and other popular study destinations. We help students choose the right course and career pathway. Our experienced counsellors assist students at every stage – course selection, applications, visa guidance, pre-departure preparation.

IELTS

We assist students in improving their confidence and performance by providing them with appropriate coaching, learning support, and exam-focused preparation for the IELTS.

Growth Story, Vision, and Strategy

Founder and Director Message

Founder and Director Message

A letter from our director

In today’s world of globalization, where national and cultural boundaries are constantly blurring, it is the responsibility of higher education institutions to develop students who can navigate the world beyond borders.

As the founder of ISMS Global Education, I feel that in this age of globalization, an academic journey beyond your home country is an excellent networking opportunity with people from across the world. Therefore, ISMS Global gives you an experience of lifetime by making it easier for students to get much-needed international exposure and developing a platform for them to do so.

We have direct partnerships with many prestigious universities in the United Kingdom, and their qualifications are globally recognized and accepted. This program helps young talents realize their dream of studying abroad and gaining work experience in the U.K. on successful completion of their respective courses. The International School of Management Studies would like to welcome you all to the family as new members.

By choosing to study with us, you will gain a good foundation for a rewarding career in the future and for your personal development.
