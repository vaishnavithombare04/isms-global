Study in the UK

Study Abroad Guide for Indian Students: Countries, Universities, Courses & Application

Experience education at its best in the UK, which is known globally for being a major education center and a source of innovative research studies.

Why Study in UK?

The UK is among the top choices of people pursuing their studies at an advanced level. People prefer studying abroad in the UK for their academic prestige and career opportunities.

Key Benefits

Top Advantages of Studying in the UK

1. Top Universities around the World

There are various top universities which have a reputation for being centers for academic excellence and innovative research studies.

2. Short-Course Study Programs

Undergraduate courses at the University take three years, while postgraduate courses usually only last one year.

3. Career Benefits

Students who graduate from UK universities have opportunities to enter the global market where there are many multinational companies.

4. Diverse Student Body

The students come from different backgrounds as there are students from all over the world in a single institution.

5. Work Opportunities After Studies

The UK Graduate Route gives international students chances to work and remain in the UK.

6. Focus on Research

UK institutions prioritize pioneering research, practical innovation, and industry exposure.

Academic Programs

Courses and Universities

In the UK, universities give priority to academic research, innovation, and practical studies along with industry exposure.

7. Cultural and International Exposure

Through interactions with the international community, the UK enables the acquisition of many important skills. The UK is the best place to undertake advanced programs in education for international students.

Best courses to pursue in the UK

The United Kingdom serves as an optimal study location for foreign students because it offers them access to its superior educational institutions and skilled teaching staff and multiple academic opportunities.

You need to understand all the popular academic programs which UK universities offer because they will assist you in achieving success in your selected career path.

  • Business management
  • MBA
  • Healthcare & Nursing
  • Engineering and technology
  • Medicine
  • Hospitality & Tourism
  • Law
  • Social sciences
  • Media and communication
  • Sports Business Management & many more

Enrollment & Eligibility

Intakes, English Tests, and Tuition Introduction

Intakes in UK

Several intakes are off:

Intake in September

Major intake with a wide range of course selections.

Intake in January

A common choice of intake for various courses.

Intake in May

Open for specific courses and institutions.

UK University Admission Requirements

They differ from one university and course to another.

Academic Requirements

Applicants need to submit:

  • Academic transcripts
  • Degree certificates
  • GPA or percentage requirements

English Language Requirements

Common English Language Test Scores include:

  • IELTS
  • TOEFL
  • PTE
  • Duolingo

Other Required Documents

  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • Letter of Recommendation (LOR)
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Passport
Certain professional courses are preferred by universities in the UK.
Financial Planning

Tuition, Living Costs, and Scholarships

Cost of Studying in the UK — Average Tuition Fees

Undergraduate Courses

GBP 10,000 to GBP 25,000 annually

Postgraduate Courses

GBP 10,000 to GBP 25,000 annually

Doctoral Degree

£15,000 to £50,000 depending on the university

Please note that all costs mentioned are approximate estimates, and PhD expenses may vary since fully funded opportunities are also available in the UK.

Students interested in Study abroad for Indian Students programs must also factor in living costs. To reduce your overall education expenses, students are encouraged to apply for the various scholarships available for Indian students.

Living Costs in the UK

The cost of living will depend on the city and the individual’s personal preferences.

Average Monthly Costs

Expense Category Estimated Monthly Range (£ GBP)
Rent £400 to £1,200
Grocery £150 to £300
Transport £50 to £150
Internet & Utilities £50 to £100
Mixed expenses £100 to £300
London and similar cities are significantly costlier than other cities in the UK. Students must plan their budgets accordingly while preparing to study abroad.

Scholarships, Visa Requirements, and Student Lifestyle

Scholarships for Foreign Students in UK

There are various scholarships available to foreign students.

Categories of Scholarships

Merit Based Scholarship

Granted according to academic merit

Need Based Scholarships

Offered students with need

Government Scholarships
  • Chevening Scholarships
  • Commonwealth Scholarships
  • University Scholarships

There are many universities in the UK offering scholarships to foreign students.

Student visa requirements for the UK

Visa name Who it’s for When to apply Arrival in the UK
Student Visa UK Students aged 16 or over who have been offered a course place in a course at a UK educational institution Three months before the start of your course Up to one week before if your course lasts six months or less. Up to one month before if your course lasts more than six months
