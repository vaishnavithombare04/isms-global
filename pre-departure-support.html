Pre-Departure and Post-Arrival Briefing

Our main objective is to provide you with all the necessary support at every step during your study abroad experience. Preparing yourself well for overseas education is important to ensure that you excel. Our counsellors will help you get ready by providing you with all the information you require.

Detailed Guidance Modules

What Does the Pre-Departure Briefing Include?

Travel and Flight Guidance

We guide students on things to do before they travel. This includes:

  • Flight booking tips
  • Baggage
  • Important papers you need for travel
  • Airport procedures
  • Immigration Guidance
  • Accommodation Guidance

Accommodation Guidance

We also help students with:

  • Student housing options
  • Temporary accommodation
  • Shared apartments and private housing
  • Safety and location considerations

Financial & Forex Guidance

We help students understand:

  • How to exchange currency and use forex services
  • How international banking works
  • Using debit and credit cards, in countries
  • Managing your expenses. Making a budget
  • We make sure students know about currency exchange and forex services
  • Students learn about banking and debit/credit card usage abroad

Health and Insurance Information

Students will be provided with advice about:

  • Requirements for health insurance
  • Medical support services
  • Emergency contacts
  • Healthcare system in the host country
Orientation & Packing Checklist

Culture, Academics, Safety, and Packing

Culture and Lifestyle Preparation

Living abroad entails adaptation to a completely new cultural environment. Therefore, we assist students with:

  • • The local culture and traditions
  • • Climate and necessary clothing items
  • • The local transport system
  • • Communication and manners
  • • Student life in a foreign country

Academic Preparation

We provide students with information about:

  • • Orientation programs at the university
  • • Classroom culture and pedagogical practices
  • • Coursework and attendance requirements
  • • Academic management and planning

Safety and Emergency Preparation

Students will be advised about:

  • • Personal safety practices
  • • Phone numbers of emergency hotlines
  • • Laws and regulations
  • • Safety during travels and abroad

Things to Pack: The "Must-Have" Packing Guide

ISMS Global Education offers a complete student pack including guidance on.

  • • Documents that need to be carried by the student
  • • Clothes required by the student
  • • Electricals and accessories that the student will need
  • • Medicines and personal belongings that the student might require
  • • Items that students ought not to pack
