Intakes in New Zealand

In some universities, intakes may also be referred to as a semester. The two intakes available in New Zealand are:

Semester 1: February to June
Semester 2: July to November

Admissions for vocational courses may also be available in the months of March, April, and May.

Frequently Asked Questions

Eligibility and English Requirements

Key admission criteria, recognized English language tests, and IELTS score benchmarks for New Zealand student visas and universities.

1. What is the eligibility condition for pursuing higher education in New Zealand? +

The following are the conditions of eligibility for different levels of education as follows:

For Undergraduate Courses:
  • Equivalent of Year 13 completion.
  • Requirement of minimum 50-60% marks or above for admissions.
  • IELTS English Language Proficiency Test
For Postgraduate Courses:
  • Requirement of minimum 50-60% marks in undergraduate courses.
  • GMAT or GRE scorecard for pursuing management course.
  • Universities might consider candidates' internship/working experience also.
  • Requirement of IELTS scorecard for English proficiency test.
2. What exam is needed to pursue higher studies in New Zealand? +

Recognized examinations for pursuing higher studies in New Zealand include:

IELTS GRE TOEFL PTE UCAT ANZ (University Clinical Aptitude Test for Australia & New Zealand)
3. What will be IELTS score for New Zealand? +

For student visa requirement to enter New Zealand, students must need at least a band score of 5.5 in IELTS examination, whereas for university admission, student must have at least overall band score of 6.0, whereas band 5.5 in each section.
