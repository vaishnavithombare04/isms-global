Study in Australia

Here’s a comprehensive guide for all Indian students who want to study in Australia

38 Top-Ranked Universities  |  22,000 Courses  |  Up to* 6 Years of PSW  |  High-Paying Jobs – Australia has it all!

Key Advantages

Some of the benefits associated with studying in Australia

Top Worldwide Cities for Students

According to the QS Best Student Cities 2022, many Australian cities are found among the top 30 best student cities in the world based on such criteria as student population, student supply, hiring rate, etc., including Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Canberra and Adelaide.

Top Universities

Numerous Australian universities have gained recognition across multiple international university rankings including, University of Melbourne, Australian National University, University of Sydney, Queensland University of Technology, and University of New South Wales based on their excellence in teaching and research and inclusion in the QS World University Rankings 2022.

Education System and Popular Courses

For six years immediately following graduation from an Australian university, Indians can obtain employment in Australia.

Australia is considered a leader in quality of life, natural beauty and well-being of its cities.

The university system in Australia provides international students with a variety of services and assistance, such as career advice, orientation programs and legal assistance.

Safe and Student-Centric Atmosphere

Life in Australia is of high quality. The facilities for students are excellent.

Research and Innovation

Australian universities excel in research and education focused on industry.

Intakes Available in Australia

Australian universities generally offer multiple intakes. In contrast with one intake in Indian universities, there are two major intakes in Australian colleges and universities (terms/semesters).

Intake 1: February / March — Major academic semester start
Intake 2: July / August — Mid-year intake option
Intake 3: September / October — Trimester / specific programs

Tuition Fees & Living Expenses in Australia

Cost of studying in Australia will depend on what level of qualification you want to acquire and what universities or schools you choose to enroll in. For example, fees for veterinary and medical courses are relatively higher compared to other courses. Tuition fees for one of the Australian universities can range from *AUD 25,000 to AUD 40,000 annually.

Immigration & Health Requirements

Australia Student Visa Requirements (Subclass 500)

You will have access to stay in Australia for the length of time it takes to finish your study, depending on how long you are enrolled and when you complete your course as of July 1, 2016, if you hold a Subclass 500 student visa for the length of time you study in Australia. Ensure you have a current visa before arriving and adhere to all the conditions given on your student visa to ensure a seamless entry. For additional information, refer to the Department of Home Affairs at the Australian Government.

Documents Needed for a Student Visa Australia

  • Passport
  • Enrolment Confirmation (CoE)
  • Academic Documents
  • English Test Scores
  • Proof of Funds
  • Health Insurance (OSHC)

Build a Global Career After Graduation

Did you know that you can reduce your cost of living and earn work experience while studying in Australia? With the help of laws prevailing in Australia, you can work part time on your student visa for 48 hours every fortnight.
