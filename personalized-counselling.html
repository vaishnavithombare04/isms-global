Comprehensive Session Coverage

What Does a One-on-One Session with ISMS Global Education Experts Include?

Our personalized counselling sessions help students understand their studies. During the session students get:

Career and profile evaluation

In-depth review of academic history, personal strengths, interests, and career ambitions.

Advice on the countries and universities

Expert recommendations tailored to global study destinations and university accreditation.

Course recommendations based on career goals

Aligning specialized academic programs with long-term industry demands and career growth.

Information on admission requirements and eligibility

Clear breakdown of grade cutoffs, language test criteria, prerequisites, and deadlines.

Scholarship and financial planning advice

Guidance on institutional bursaries, merit scholarships, tuition costs, and budgeting.

Visa guidance and application process support

Step-by-step visa documentation checks, financial proof structuring, and application assistance.

Details about post-study work opportunities

Insights into post-study work permits, post-graduation visas, and international job markets.

A personalized study abroad plan

A tailored roadmap with milestones from application submission to pre-departure departure.

Our counsellors make sure each student gets guidance that fits their goals and preferences. They help students make decisions about their career and education.

Session Readiness

Tips for Optimum Benefit of Individual Counselling Session

To maximize your benefit from personal counselling by our team of study abroad experts, you must do the following:

1
Do thorough research before visiting the counsellor
2
Prepare a list of questions and concerns
3
Know your objectives in life and your budget
4
Have an honest discussion about your education background
5
Discuss scholarship issues freely
6
Be open-minded regarding opportunities abroad
7
Note down important information for future use
This will allow you to get maximum benefit out of the session
Document Checklist

What Documents Should I Bring?

Bring the following documents/information during your session:

Academic transcripts & mark sheets
Photocopy of passport (if available)
Updated resume/CV
English test score (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL)
Work experience document (if applicable)
List of preferred countries and courses
Inquiries about admissions, scholarships, visas and careers
Your counsellor will be better prepared to offer advice if you have these ready.
